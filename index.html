Industries We Serve

Discover how our flexible packaging solutions cater to specific product categories and manufacturing needs.

Food

Food industry has changed tremendously based on consumer preferences and aspirations. This creates demanding requirements on packaging in terms of moisture barriers, integrity, shelf-life, and hygiene.

  • Rice and Dhall Packaging
  • Atta & Maida Flour, Rava Bags
  • Bread & Bakery Packaging (LD Bags)
  • Salt & Grocery Packaging (HM Bags)
  • Milk & Dairy Liquid Pouches
  • Fruit Juices & Liquid Barrier Pouches
  • Spices, Masala & Seasoning Pouches
View More

Snacks

Aesthetic packaging plays a vital role in retail snacks. With customers looking for premium, fresh products, high-barrier printed pouches preserve crunchiness, protect aromas, and enhance shelf appeal.

  • Savouries and Namkeen Packaging
  • Banana Chips & Potato Wafers
  • Murukku & Traditional Mixture Bags
  • Rusk & Biscuit Packaging Bags
  • Groundnut Chikki & Sweet Pouches
  • Extruded Snack Pouches
View More

Garments

If the garments industry is all about making people look better, our packaging is all about making those garments look stunning on retail shelves, with superior gloss, clarity, and protection.

  • Shirt & Apparel Display Bags
  • Saree & Textile Packaging Bags
  • BOPP Self-Adhesive Clear Bags
  • Garment Bags with Hanger Holes
  • Innerwear & Hosiery Packaging
  • Soft PVC & LD Zippered Textile Bags
View More

Agro & Industrial

Agricultural and industrial products require rugged protection for open-air storage, transport, and handling. We manufacture robust, puncture-resistant bags and continuous specialty film rolls.

  • Fertilizer & Chemical Heavy-Duty Bags
  • Seed Bags & Agro Packaging
  • Form-Fill-Seal Continuous LD Rolls
  • High-Barrier Specialty Film Rolls
  • Heavy-Duty Industrial Liners
View More

Others

In addition to food and textiles, we supply custom packaging for high-volume retail essentials, religious items, detergents, stationery, and hardware, customized to demanding barrier needs.

  • Detergent Powder Stand-up Pouches
  • Viboothi & Pooja Offering Packaging
  • Transparent PVC Zipper Pouches
  • Fireworks & Matches Packaging
  • Cosmetics, Stationery & Hardware Bags
  • Custom Multi-Color Printed Packaging
